# Support Guidelines for pfCentral

Welcome to the pfCentral project! Below are details on community resources and professional commercial support options.

---

## Free Community Support

Community support is provided on a best-effort, volunteer basis by open-source maintainers and fellow community members:

- **GitHub Discussions**: General questions, architecture sharing, deployment advice, and self-hosting discussions.
- **GitHub Issues**: Verified bug reports and actionable feature proposals.
- **Documentation**: Comprehensive setup and maintenance guides in the [`docs/`](docs/) directory.

*Note: Free community support does not include guaranteed response times, deployment troubleshooting for private networks, or custom driver development.*

---

## Commercial Support, Customization & MSP Services

For enterprise deployments requiring guaranteed SLAs, emergency escalation, custom development, or hands-off managed operations:

- **Dedicated Deployment Assistance**: On-premise or cloud installation, High Availability (HA) architecture, and database clustering.
- **Managed Service Provider (MSP) Retainers**: Proactive 24/7 telemetry monitoring, scheduled pfSense patch management, and automated disaster recovery testing.
- **Custom Integration**: Custom SIEM / Splunk / ELK integrations, webhooks, and enterprise ticket system connectors.
- **Firewall Policy & Security Audits**: Rule optimization, CARP cluster validation, and WireGuard/IPsec WAN design.
